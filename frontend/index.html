Hostlur User Manual & Workflow Guide 📖

Everything you need to know about using Hostlur as a Student or Warden.

1. How to Report an Issue & Attach Photos ▲

1. Navigate to + Report Issue from the top navigation bar.

2. Your Hostel Block, Floor, and Room are automatically populated from your verified student profile.

3. If the problem is in a common washroom or corridor, uncheck "My Room" to enter the custom location.

4. Attach Photo: Click the camera dropzone to upload an image of the leak, broken fixture, or hazard.

5. Click ✨ Auto-Analyze with Gemma 4 Vision. The model analyzes both the visual evidence and your text to suggest category, priority, and summary before saving!

2. Dual Resolution Verification Workflow ▼

In traditional hostels, maintenance staff frequently mark tickets as "Resolved" without checking if the repair actually fixed the root problem. Hostlur enforces Dual Verification:

• Step 1: The technician visits and the Warden marks the ticket as "Pending Student Confirmation".

• Step 2: A golden banner appears on the student's dashboard: "Warden marked this issue resolved. Please inspect."

• Step 3: If the fix works, the student clicks ✅ Confirm Resolved and gives a 1–5 star rating.

• Step 4: If the faucet is still dripping or the Wi-Fi still disconnects, the student clicks ❌ Still Broken / Reopen, which instantly reopens the ticket with an escalation note for the warden!

3. Emergency Safety Hazard Protocol ▼

Hostlur utilizes a deterministic safety engine that scans all incoming reports for critical physical and electrical hazards (burning smells, sparking sockets, exposed wires, gas leaks, ceiling cracks).

When a hazard keyword is detected:

• The ticket priority is automatically elevated to Urgent.

• A prominent 🚨 Hazard Banner is attached to the ticket.

• It appears immediately at the top of the Warden's triage queue for mandatory priority inspection.

4. Warden Portal & Student Account Management ▼

Authorized wardens have access to campus-wide triage:

• Status Management: Change status from Submitted → Under Review → In Progress → Pending Student Confirmation.

• Student Directory: Register new student accounts with verified room allocations.

• Secure Password Reset: Wardens can generate single-use temporary passwords for students who have forgotten their credentials.

5. Theme Switching & Profile Settings ▼

• Dark/Light Theme: Click the sun/moon icon in the top right navbar to seamlessly switch between the deep carbon black theme and crisp linen light theme. Your preference is saved across sessions.

• Persona Switcher: During hackathon evaluations, use the top navbar dropdown to instantly switch between Student Aarav, Student Priya, and Chief Warden Dr. Sharma.
